# Privacy Policy for Gyan Jyoti

**Effective Date:** May 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Gyan Jyoti app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed as an educational tool for children (featuring learning materials, quizzes, arts and crafts, and stories). We take privacy very seriously, especially concerning children.

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information or location data from our users.**

The Application may use third-party services (such as Google Play Services) that may collect information used to identify you for basic crash reporting or analytics, depending on your device settings.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application is intended for children. Because we do not collect any personal information (such as names, addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers), we are in full compliance with the requirements of COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## App Permissions

The Application may request certain permissions (such as microphone or storage) solely to enable core features within the app (like interactive learning activities or saving your drawings). None of this data is transmitted off your device or stored on our servers. All processing happens locally on your device.
